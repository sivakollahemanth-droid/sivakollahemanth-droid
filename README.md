## 🚀 About Me

I'm **Siva Hemanth Kolla**, a **Generative AI Research Scientist** and **ServiceNow Developer** specializing in enterprise AI transformation. My expertise spans **Generative AI**, **LLMs**, **RAG architectures**, **AI agents**, and **cloud-native automation platforms**. I'm passionate about building secure, scalable, and governance-driven AI solutions that help organizations streamline operations and accelerate innovation. I've authored multiple research publications and patents focused on responsible enterprise AI and intelligent automation.

- 🔭 I'm currently working on **Generative AI & LLM-powered enterprise solutions**
- 🌱 I'm deep-diving into **RAG architectures, AI Agents, and Multi-modal LLMs**
- 📝 I regularly write articles on [Medium](https://medium.com/@siva.kolla.hemanth) about AI, ServiceNow & automation
- 🎓 Research published on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5E8F4oMAAAAJ&hl=en)
- 💼 5+ years of experience in **ServiceNow, AI/ML, and enterprise automation**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I turn complex enterprise problems into elegant AI-powered workflows!
